07 · FAQ

Understand the ecosystem.

Is MILAN only a social media product?

No. Social is one experience. The broader direction is a Web5 ecosystem for identity, data, privacy, permission and connected digital services.

What does DID mean here?

DID refers to decentralized identity. In the Milan model it represents a foundation for a digital identity that is less dependent on a single platform account.

What does DWN mean here?

DWN refers to Decentralized Web Node architecture. In the Milan model it represents a user-centered data layer for digital information and context.

What is the long-term ecosystem goal?

The long-term goal is to connect identity, data and digital experiences while giving people clearer ownership, privacy and permission choices.
