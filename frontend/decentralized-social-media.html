01 · The foundation

One ecosystem. Five foundations.

The Web5 architecture matters because it changes the relationship between people, identity, data and digital services.

IDENTITY

Know who you are.

DID provides a decentralized identity concept for a digital presence that is not defined only by one platform login.

OWNERSHIP

Keep your data close.

DWN provides a model for a user-centered data layer where personal information can have clearer ownership and context.

PRIVACY

Privacy by design.

Personal information should not automatically become the price of participating in a digital ecosystem.

PERMISSION

Share intentionally.

Access should be understandable, visible and based on the choices that people make.

DIGITAL VALUE

Participate in value.

Milan's long-term direction is to explore transparent, consent-based models that let people participate in value created from their digital lives.

ECOSYSTEM

More than one experience.

Communication, profiles, media, AI-assisted tools and future services can become experiences inside the same Web5 foundation.

06 · FAQ

Understand the Milan model.

Is Milan a social media platform?

Social and communication features can be part of Milan, but the broader direction is a Web5 ecosystem centered on identity, data, privacy, permission and digital ownership.

What does DID mean here?

DID refers to decentralized identity—a way to represent a digital identity independently of a single platform.

What does DWN mean here?

DWN refers to Decentralized Web Node architecture, used here as the concept for a user-centered data layer.

Can people earn from their data?

Milan's long-term vision includes exploring transparent, consent-based digital value models. Specific earning mechanisms should only be described as available when they are actually implemented.
