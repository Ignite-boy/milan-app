06 · FAQ

Understand the model.

Is Milan only a decentralized social media app?

No. Social media is an important experience, but the broader direction is a Web5 ecosystem spanning identity, data, privacy, permission and future digital services.

What does DID mean here?

DID means Decentralized Identifier. In the Milan model, it represents a foundation for a digital identity that is less dependent on a single platform account.

What does DWN mean here?

DWN means Decentralized Web Node. In the Milan model, it represents a user-centered data layer for digital information and records.

Is every future ecosystem feature already available?

No. This page separates the current ecosystem direction from future possibilities. A feature should be presented as available only when it is actually implemented.
