# MILAN — Rankable Long-Tail Keywords + Growth Notes

The trick: **don't chase "social media" or "Milan"** — those are impossible to rank.
Chase long, specific phrases where the searcher already wants exactly what you offer.
Your easiest traffic wedge is **free music** (high search volume, clear intent), so half
the list below leads with that, and pulls people into the privacy/social product once they arrive.

---

## Group 1 — Music (your traffic wedge: easier to rank, high intent)

These should target your **/music** page (and music-focused blog posts / landing pages).

| # | Keyword (long-tail) | Why it's winnable |
|---|---------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | free bollywood music app no ads | Specific + "no ads" intent; big brands run ads, so this phrase is open |
| 2 | play hollywood songs online free without signup | "without signup" filters out Spotify/YouTube Music |
| 3 | ad free music streaming app india | Geo + "ad free" = low competition, real demand |
| 4 | listen to arijit singh songs free online | Artist long-tail; repeat for 8–10 top artists as separate pages |
| 5 | free lo-fi music player no login | Niche audience that actively searches this |
| 6 | bollywood song search and play free online | Matches exactly what your search box does |
| 7 | privacy friendly music app no tracking | Bridges music → your privacy story |

> Tip: turn #4 into a **template** — one lightweight page per popular artist
> ("free [artist] songs online"). 10 artists = 10 ranking pages, near-zero effort.

## How to actually use this (priority order)

1. **One page = one keyword.** Don't stuff all 15 on the homepage. Give each its own
   page/post with the phrase in the `<title>`, the `<h1>`, the URL slug, and the first paragraph.
2. **Music pages first.** They rank faster and bring volume. Use them to funnel signups.
3. **Write 300–600 real words per page** answering the searcher's question. Thin pages don't rank.
4. **Internal links:** every music page should link to the homepage value prop, and vice-versa.
5. **Get 3–5 real backlinks** (Reddit, a directory, a Product Hunt-style launch, a blog mention).
   Without any backlinks, even perfect pages rank slowly.
6. **Be patient:** SEO takes 6–12 weeks to move. Pair it with short-form video (Reels/Shorts)
   demos of "free music, no ads, you own your data" for faster early traffic.
