05 · FAQ

Understand the Milan model.

Is Milan a social media platform?

Social and communication experiences can be part of Milan, but the broader product direction is a Web5 ecosystem built around identity, data, privacy, permission and digital ownership.

What does DID mean here?

DID represents a decentralized identity concept that can make a digital identity less dependent on one platform account.

What does DWN mean here?

DWN refers to Decentralized Web Node architecture and represents a user-centered model for storing and accessing digital information.

Can people earn from their data?

Exploring transparent, consent-based digital value models is part of Milan's long-term direction. Specific earning mechanisms should only be presented as available when they are actually implemented.
