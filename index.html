How it works

Decentralized under the hood, effortless on the surface

Create your identity

Sign up in seconds — MILAN generates your own Decentralized Identifier (DID) behind the scenes. No crypto knowledge needed.

Get your data space

A personal Decentralized Web Node (DWN) is provisioned just for you. It's your private vault for everything you create.

Connect & share

Post, chat, and follow your people. You decide exactly what is shared, with whom, and for how long.

Stay in control

Your data never leaves your control. Export it, delete it, or take it with you — the platform can't hold it hostage.
