04 — Why this matters

From Web1 to Web2 to Web3 — and toward Web5.

Web1 gave people access to information. Web2 made the internet interactive and social, but it also concentrated enormous amounts of identity and data inside platforms. Web3 pushed ownership, decentralization and digital assets forward.

MILAN's Web5 vision focuses on bringing identity and personal data closer to the individual while keeping everyday digital experiences simple. The goal is not another isolated app. It is an ecosystem where people can connect, create, communicate and participate with more control over their digital lives.

05 — The economic direction

Digital participation should create value for people.

Milan's long-term direction is to explore models in which people can participate more directly in the value created by their digital presence. Any such model should be transparent, consent-based and clear about what is being shared and why.
